In short

rtex is the engine Nei uses to make INEMA reels (short vertical videos) with his own face: his HeyGen avatar speaks the script, and the engine draws the screens, word-by-word captions, and end card over it. Each video covers a single topic in a fixed format: a strong hook in the opening frame, the method’s steps in “N steps,” and, at the end, an opinion question for the audience to answer in the comments. A validator, an automatic critic, and QA check the format before publication. The code is open for study and adaptation; to generate your own video, you need your own HeyGen avatar, as well as Python, Node, ffmpeg, and Whisper.

What it is

A fixed format with the real presenter

The top two-thirds show the content; the bottom third shows Nei speaking throughout the entire reel. Every number has a source on screen, and yellow appears as a surface (a yellow background) only once: on the pill with the final question’s options. Highlighted words in yellow type, such as AGENT in the headline, do not count.

🎙️ Nei’s real avatar

The voiceover goes to the HeyGen studio through the subscription; the avatar video comes back, Whisper timestamps each word, and the engine fits the screens and captions to that timing.

🧩 Step-based format

One topic: hook in frame 0, evidence by 20 s, the viewer’s problem, “N steps” with a visible index, evidence, caveat, maxim after a silence, and an opinion question.

✅ Three checks

The validator enforces the rules; a critic (claude -p) scores the script; QA measures duration, pacing, volume, screen changes per minute, silence, end card, and yellow.

The format

The nine blocks of the rtex format

One topic per video, in this order. The checar command rejects the script if a required block is missing or out of place. A video with five different topics (a list) will not pass: “N steps” means the steps in the method for a single topic.

  1. thesis: the hook (frame 0 and the first 3 seconds), described below.
  2. authority: evidence on screen by 20 s: a number with a source (the counter lands on the source’s value) or an authority, also with a source.
  3. problem: the viewer’s pain point, right after the evidence, with reassurance in the spoken line (“it’s not your fault”) and an object (in the sample, a chat being crossed out).
  4. turn: announce “N steps”; the 01–N index appears before the first step and becomes pills at the top.
  5. method: numbered steps, one object per step, label “0N / NAME.”
  1. evidence: a sourced fact that confirms what came before.
  2. caveat: limitations, spoken and readable, turned into an instruction.
  3. maxim: a short antithesis after 0.5–0.9 s of silence (voice and music disappear).
  4. cta: the opinion question (below); the card stays up for 9 s after the last word.

The hook: frame 0 and the first 3 seconds

Four types, and only these

gancho.tipo field: contrario (tell people to stop doing something everyone does: “STOP CREATING AGENTS”), perda (what if something disappeared?), estatistica_medo (a scary number, with a source on screen), or historia_terceiro (someone else’s story, with a number and a source). Forbidden: lists (“5 steps this month,” “N things…”), “did you know…” and “I did…”.

A headline stronger than the voice

The frame 0 headline (tela.linhas) has up to 6 words, in uppercase, with one yellow word (tela.destaque). It is more aggressive than the spoken line: the text stops people scrolling with the sound off; the voice softens and explains. It is already complete in frame 0, which serves as the cover. Below it, a metaphor object with damage (objeto.params.metafora; today, the only accepted value is "fio") changes about every 1 s during the first 3 seconds.

The hook at the end: an opinion question

What it is

The video ends with a strong, emotional, opinionated question tied to the thesis, which people answer with their own view: by choosing one of two or three words (“ONE or MANY?”) or sharing their own experience. Nei states his side first to provoke a response. The question’s tension appears in frame 0 and returns at the end.

What is not allowed

Promising anything to people who comment. No “I’ll send it,” “I’ll share it,” “send me the link,” “link in bio,” “comment X and…” or “it’s in the pinned comment.” No one is expecting a reply, so Nei does not need to respond to anyone. Asking people to “comment ONE or MANY” is allowed; what is not allowed is tying the comment to a reward. Links and sources can sit in the post’s pinned comment.

Quick glossary

Frame 0

The video’s first frame. It already shows the complete headline and serves as the cover.

Hook

The request for a comment at the end. In rtex, it is an opinion question (the script’s isca field); the options appear in the video’s only yellow pill.

Maxim

The closing summary line, phrased as an antithesis (“She isn’t stupid. She just doesn’t know your work.”), after a short silence.

Stage

The motor/palco.html page that draws the screens. The render records this page at 30 frames per second.

HeyGen and studio

HeyGen is the service that generates the avatar video. rtex uses the studio (the website, through a subscription) in a browser open on the virtual display :99.

Karaoke captions

Captions light up word by word in sync with speech, with keywords in color.

LUFS

A measure of perceived loudness. −14 LUFS is the volume level social networks use as a standard.

CTA

“Call to action”: the video’s final prompt. Here there is just one: the opinion question.

Whisper and Playwright

Whisper transcribes the voice and timestamps each word. Playwright opens the stage in a browser and captures every frame.

Critic and reviewer

The critic is a claude -p that scores each script block. The independent reviewer is another session that only looks at the result.

QA and contact sheet

QA (“quality assurance”) is the command that measures the finished video. The contact sheet is the image it builds with 16 video frames, so everything can be checked at once.

Pill

A rounded label. The numbered pills at the top (01–03 in the sample script) show which step the video is on; the yellow pill holds the final question’s options.

Bot and queue

Only on Nei’s machine: the bot is the Telegram robot that receives the video for him to watch; the queue publishes one video every 2 hours to the YouTube channel named in the script.

How it works

From script to video.mp4

rtex.py runs each step. The avatar is the slow part: the studio can take minutes or hours to deliver the video.

roteiro.json→ checar→ critico→ avatar-enviar (HeyGen)→ prints→ avatar + Whisper→ cena→ render (30 fps, −14 LUFS)→ qa→ video.mp4
1

Validated script

checar checks the hook, evidence by 20 s, a single topic, block order, index = method, spoken lines without numerals or “!”, a source for every number, an opinion question with no promise, and an estimated duration of 60–90 s. critico gives it a score.

2

Avatar and timing

The speech goes to the studio; the video comes back, Whisper timestamps the words, the speech (HeyGen speaks quickly) is stretched to about 182 words/min (by no more than 12%), and silence is added before the maxim.

3

Scene, render, and QA

cena links each screen to a word in the speech; render records the stage, places the avatar below, and adds the music; qa measures everything and builds the contact sheet.

Requirements

What needs to be on the machine

The engine was built for Nei’s Linux machine. The paths below are in the code; on a different machine, use the listed variables or adjust the file.

Avatar: HeyGen studio

A HeyGen account with your avatar and voice. The studio opens in a browser window, so it needs a display: the virtual display :99 (Xvfb). One-time setup: (1) log in to a Playwright browser profile; (2) in the studio, create a video project named exactly TEMPLATE-AVATAR16 (or another name in RTEX_HEYGEN_TEMPLATE), with your avatar and voice: the engine opens that project, chooses “edit as new,” and replaces the speech; (3) put your HeyGen API key in the file the downloader reads (fixed path in heygen/heygen-estudio-baixar.mjs; it only checks the status and downloads the video).

npx playwright open --user-data-dir ~/.cache/inemaccbot/perfil-heygen https://app.heygen.com   # login; or another dir in RTEX_HEYGEN_PERFIL
mkdir -p ~/projetos/openpcbotv2
echo "HEYGEN_API_KEY=sua-chave" >> ~/projetos/openpcbotv2/.env

Whisper (inemavox)

inematds/inemavox in ~/projetos/inemavox (or another path in CS_INEMAVOX). rtex tries the Whisper service on port 7871 and, if it is unavailable, runs inemavox’s transcrever_v1.py with large-v3 (about 10 GB of NVIDIA GPU memory). The resident Whisper Python comes from CS_WHISPER_PY; the default points to a chatterbox conda environment on Nei’s machine, so point it to yours.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/inemavox ~/projetos/inemavox
# dependencies: see the inemavox README
export CS_WHISPER_PY=/path/to/your/python3

Node, ffmpeg, Python

Node 18+, ffmpeg with ffprobe, Python 3 with Pillow, numpy, and cairosvg, the DejaVu font (the fonts-dejavu-core package, used for the name label), and the claude CLI logged in for the critic. Product logos (e.g., Claude’s in the sample script) come from an open package that ferramentas/logos.sh downloads once to ~/.cache/r7ex/lobe-icons; without it, the prints step stops.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/rtex
cd rtex
npm i
npx playwright install chromium
pip install --user pillow numpy cairosvg   # or inside a venv
bash ferramentas/logos.sh                  # logos -> ~/.cache/r7ex/lobe-icons
Xvfb :99 -screen 0 1920x1080x24 &   # only for the HeyGen studio
Shortest path, without the studio: if you already have a 16:9 (1920×1080) video of yourself or your avatar speaking the script, skip HeyGen entirely: python3 rtex.py fazer <pasta> --de seu-video.mp4. The video’s speech must match the script at least 85% (the engine checks with Whisper). This path does not need HeyGen, a key, or Xvfb. Whisper listens in Portuguese; for a reel in another language, use CS_LANG=en or CS_LANG=es.
Optional: enviar sends the video to Nei’s Telegram bot (bin/enviar_bot_v3.mjs) and fila adds the video to r7ex’s publishing queue (~/projetos/r7ex/fila/fila.py, or RTEX_FILA). Away from Nei’s machine, stop at qa and publish however you prefer.
User guide · step by step

From topic to checked video

Each edition lives in edicoes/<data>-<slug>/. The fazer shortcut (step 9) runs checar, prints, avatar, cena, render, and qa in one go.

1

Create the edition you

Copy the sample script (one topic: “one agent and one folder per task”) to a new folder dated today. The sample’s sources were checked on 28/10/2026, and checar rejects sources older than 7 days: after 04/11/2026, the untouched sample will intentionally fail (this is not a bug). Replace the sources with your own in step 2.

python3 rtex.py novo minha-edicao   # -> edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao/roteiro.json
2

Write the script with sources you

Check every number against its source that day and record it in fontes[] with valor and verificado_em. Then write the hook, the blocks (format in “The roteiro.json file”), the full spoken lines, and the hook question.

3

Validate and run the critic

checar applies fixed rules. critico asks a claude -p running under the subscription to score it: it passes with no blockers, a thesis (the hook) score ≥ 8, an average ≥ 7.5, and no block below 6. It writes critica.json.

python3 rtex.py checar  edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py critico edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
4

Approve and send the avatar you

The upload only proceeds with written approval for this production in avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN (one avatar per reel). After submission, the speech is frozen: text errors are fixed in the captions (the script’s trocas field), not by generating a second avatar.

mkdir -p edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao/avatar
echo "ok para este reel" > edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
python3 rtex.py avatar-enviar edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao   # writes avatar/video_id.txt
5

Capture page screenshots

Real screenshots of the cited GitHub pages and the logos. Open and read the screenshots before continuing.

python3 rtex.py prints edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
6

Preview without the avatar optional

While the studio is working, use a copy of the folder: synthetic timings, a scene, and a few individual frames (in seconds). Catch layout errors before the avatar arrives. The preview does not call the studio or use another avatar.

rm -rf /tmp/previa && cp -r edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao /tmp/previa
python3 ferramentas/tempos_sinteticos.py /tmp/previa
python3 rtex.py cena /tmp/previa
node motor/render.mjs /tmp/previa --quadros 1,10,20   # -> /tmp/previa/q_1.jpg, q_10.jpg, q_20.jpg
7

Download the avatar and adjust the pacing

avatar-conferir checks once (ready or processing). avatar waits, downloads, timestamps the words with Whisper, stretches the speech to about 182 words/min (HeyGen speaks quickly), adds silence before the maxim, and holds the card for 9 s.

python3 rtex.py avatar-conferir edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py avatar edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py avatar edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao --de avatar.mp4   # reuse an mp4
8

Scene, render, and QA

The stage at 30 fps, avatar in the bottom third, music, and −14 LUFS in two passes; QA writes build/qa.json and the contact sheet build/folha.jpg with 16 frames.

python3 rtex.py cena   edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py render edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao   # -> video.mp4
python3 rtex.py qa     edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
9

Or do it all at once

checar → prints → avatar → cena → render → qa. It does not run critico (step 3). Without --de and without a ready avatar, it sends the video to the studio itself and waits up to about 290 min for HeyGen; for this, the step 4 avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN file must exist, or it stops with that notice. With --de, HeyGen is not needed, but Whisper, the logos, and internet access for GitHub screenshots are still required.

python3 rtex.py fazer edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py fazer edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao --de seu-video.mp4   # without the studio
10

Review, revise, and deliver you

Read the contact sheet and qa.json; ask someone who did not write the script to review it. The hook promises nothing, so there is nothing to prepare for commenters. On Nei’s machine, enviar sends it to the bot and fila adds it to the channel queue (never direct upload).

python3 rtex.py enviar edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py fila   edicoes/YYYY-MM-DD-minha-edicao
The roteiro.json file

The script format, at a glance

Excerpt from the sample script, shortened (the // lines are not valid JSON; the full file is in modelo/roteiro.json).

{
  "versao": "rtex/1", "tema": "Para de criar agente: um agente só e uma pasta por tarefa",
  "gancho": { "tipo": "contrario" },
  "isca": { "tipo": "opiniao", "pergunta": "Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa?",
            "opcoes": ["UM", "VÁRIOS"], "lado": "um agente só", "tensao": "agente" },
  "fontes": [ { "id": "skills", "url": "https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/agents-and-tools/agent-skills", "valor": "uma pasta por tarefa", "verificado_em": "2026-10-28" } ],
  "trocas": { "é cê cê": "ECC", "três peças": "3 peças" },
  "blocos": [
    { "tipo": "tese", "fala": "Se você cria um agente para cada tarefa, eu tenho uma má notícia: o fio que liga todos eles vai arrebentar.",
      "tela": { "linhas": ["PARA DE", "CRIAR AGENTE."], "destaque": "AGENTE" },
      "objeto": { "comp": "tese", "params": { "metafora": "fio", "nos": ["✉️ e-mail", "📷 Instagram", "📄 propostas"] } } },
    // ... authority (source: skills), problem ("not your fault"), turning point ...
    { "tipo": "metodo", "n": 1, "nome": "PROCESSO", "fonte": "skills",
      "fala": "Um: o processo. Peça para a IA te entrevistar, uma pergunta por vez, até ela fazer sem você.",
      "objeto": { "comp": "pasta", "params": { "titulo": "O processo" } } },
    // ... method 2–3, evidence, caveat, takeaway ...
    { "tipo": "cta", "fala": "Eu já escolhi o meu lado: um agente só. E você? Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa? Comenta um ou vários.",
      "objeto": { "comp": "isca", "params": { "em": "Comenta" } } }
  ],
  "publicar": { "descricao": "... Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa?" }
}

Main fields

tipo (the order of the nine blocks) · fala (spelled out, no numerals or “!”) · tela (large text and highlight) · objeto.comp + objeto.params (what the stage draws) · n and nome in method items (matching the turn index) · fonte (an id in fontes). At the top level: trocas (how speech appears in captions), cores (colored words), ritmo, and publicar (title, description, queue channel). checar also requires: a valid gancho.tipo and a frame 0 headline of up to 6 uppercase words; objeto.params.metafora in the thesis; for the maxim, linha1 and linha2 with up to 7 words each; sources checked no more than 7 days ago; and in isca: a pergunta ending in “?” (up to 70 characters) and spoken in the CTA, 2 or 3 one-word uppercase opcoes spoken in the CTA (for an open-ended question: exemplo, the answer the hand types), lado, tensao (a word from the question that appears in frame 0 and returns at the end), and publicar.descricao ending with the question. The old fields isca.palavra and isca.entregavel cause an error.

Stage components (objeto.comp)

tese, contador, chat_riscado, indice (with progress pills), pasta, repo (real screenshot + stars), manchete, selo_prova, ressalva, maxima (typographic flash), and isca (the question, yellow pill with options, and comment box). The tese with metafora: "fio" draws the object that breaks in frame 0.
