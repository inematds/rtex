In brief

rtex is the engine Nei uses to make INEMA reels (short vertical videos) featuring his own face: his HeyGen avatar speaks the script, and the engine overlays screens, word-by-word captions, and the end card. Each video covers a single topic, in a fixed format: a strong hook in the opening frame, the method steps in “N pieces,” and, at the end, an opinion question for the audience to answer in the comments. A validator, an automated critic, and QA check the format before publication. The code is open to study and adapt; to generate your own video, you need your own HeyGen avatar, as well as Python, Node, ffmpeg, and Whisper.

What it is

A fixed format, with the real presenter

The top two thirds show the content; the bottom third shows Nei speaking throughout the reel. Every number has a source on screen, and yellow appears as a surface only once: in the pill with the final question’s options.

🎙️ Nei’s real avatar

The speech goes to the HeyGen studio through the subscription; the avatar video comes back, Whisper timestamps every word, and the engine fits screens and captions to that timing.

🧩 Piece-by-piece format

One topic: hook in frame 0, proof by 20 s, the viewer’s problem, “N pieces” (the steps) with a visible index, proof, caveat, a maxim after a silence, and an opinion question.

✅ Three checks

The validator enforces the rule; a critic (claude -p) scores the script; QA measures duration, pacing, volume, screen changes per minute, silence, the end card, and yellow.

The format

The nine blocks in the rtex format

One topic per video, in this order. The checar command rejects the script if a required block is missing or out of order. A video with five different subjects (a list) won’t pass: the “N pieces” are the steps in the method for a single topic.

  1. thesis: the hook (frame 0 and the first 3 seconds), detailed below.
  2. authority: proof on screen by 20 s: a number with a source (the counter lands on the source’s value) or an authority, also with a source.
  3. problem: the viewer’s pain point, right after the proof, absolved in the spoken line (“não é culpa sua” (it’s not your fault)) and shown with an object (in the template, a chat being crossed out).
  4. turn: “N pieces” announced; the 01–N index appears before the first step and becomes pills at the top.
  5. method: numbered steps, one object per step, label “0N / NAME.”
  1. proof: a fact with a source that confirms what came before.
  2. caveat: the limitations, spoken and readable, turned into an instruction.
  3. maxim: a short antithesis, after 0.5–0.9 s of silence (voice and music disappear).
  4. cta: the opinion question (below); the card stays on screen for 9 s after the last word.

The hook: frame 0 and the first 3 seconds

Four types, and only these

Field gancho.tipo: contrario (tells people to stop doing something everyone does: “PARA DE CRIAR AGENTE” (STOP CREATING AGENTS)), perda (what if something disappeared?), estatistica_medo (a startling number, with a source on screen), or historia_terceiro (someone else’s story, with a number and source). Forbidden: lists (“5 pieces of the month,” “N things…”), “did you know that…,” and “I did…”.

A headline stronger than the voice

The frame 0 headline (tela.linhas) has up to 6 words, in uppercase, with one yellow word (tela.destaque). It’s more aggressive than the spoken line: the text holds the attention of people scrolling with the sound off, while the voice softens and explains. It’s already in frame 0, which serves as the cover. Below it, a metaphor object with damage (objeto.params.metafora, currently "fio") changes about every 1 s during the first 3 seconds.

The hook: an opinion question

What it is

The video ends with a strong, emotional, opinionated question tied to the thesis, which people answer with their own opinion: by choosing one of two or three words (“UM ou VÁRIOS?” (ONE or SEVERAL?)) or sharing their own experience. Nei states his side first to provoke a response. The question’s tension appears in frame 0 and returns at the end.

What’s not allowed

Don’t promise anything to people who comment. No “te mando” (I’ll send it to you), “eu envio” (I’ll send it), “manda o link” (send the link), “link na bio” (link in bio), “comenta X que…” (comment X and…), or “está no comentário fixado” (it’s in the pinned comment). No one expects a reply, so Nei doesn’t have to respond to anyone. Links and sources can stay in the post’s pinned comment.

Quick glossary

Hook question

The request for a comment at the end. In rtex, it’s an opinion question (the script’s isca field); the options appear in the video’s only yellow pill.

Maxim

The summary line at the end, phrased as an antithesis (“She isn’t stupid. She just doesn’t know your work.”), after a short silence.

Stage

The motor/palco.html page that draws the screens. The render records this page at 30 frames per second.

HeyGen and the studio

HeyGen is the service that generates the video of the speaking avatar. rtex uses the studio (the website, through the subscription) in a browser opened on the virtual display :99.

Karaoke captions

Captions light up word by word in time with the speech, with keywords in color.

LUFS

A measure of perceived loudness. −14 LUFS is the volume social networks use as a standard.

CTA

“Call to action”: the video’s final prompt. Here there’s only one: the opinion question.

Whisper and Playwright

Whisper transcribes the voice and timestamps every word. Playwright opens the stage in a browser and records each frame.

Critic and reviewer

The critic is a claude -p that scores each script block. The independent reviewer is another session that only looks at the result.

QA and contact sheet

QA (“quality assurance”) is the command that measures the finished video. The contact sheet is the image it creates with 16 frames from the video, so everything can be checked at once.

Pill

A rounded label. The 01–05 pills at the top show which piece the video is on; the yellow pill contains the final question’s options.

Bot and queue

Only on Nei’s machine: the bot is the Telegram robot that receives the video for him to watch; the queue publishes a video every 2 h to the YouTube channel specified in the script.

How it works

From script to video.mp4

rtex.py runs each step. The avatar is the slow part: the studio may take minutes to hours to deliver the video.

roteiro.json→ checar→ critico→ avatar-enviar (HeyGen)→ prints→ avatar + Whisper→ cena→ render (30 fps, −14 LUFS)→ qa→ video.mp4
1

Script checked

checar enforces the hook, proof by 20 s, a single topic, block order, index = method, speech with no numerals or “!”, a source for every number, an opinion question with no promise, and an estimated duration of 60–90 s. critico assigns the score.

2

Avatar and timing

The speech goes to the studio; the video comes back, Whisper timestamps the words, and the speech (HeyGen speaks quickly) is stretched to about 182 words/min (12% maximum), with the silence before the maxim added.

3

Scene, render, and QA

cena links each screen to a spoken word; render records the stage, places the avatar at the bottom, and adds the music; qa measures everything and creates the contact sheet.

Prerequisites

What needs to be on the machine

The engine was built for Nei’s machine (Linux). The paths below are in the code; on another machine, use the mentioned variables or adjust the file.

Avatar: HeyGen studio

A HeyGen account with your avatar and voice. The studio opens in a browser window, so you need a display: the virtual display :99 (Xvfb). One-time setup: (1) log in to a Playwright browser profile; (2) in the studio, create a video project with the exact name TEMPLATE-AVATAR16 (or another name in RTEX_HEYGEN_TEMPLATE), with your avatar and voice: the engine opens this project, uses “edit as new,” and replaces the speech; (3) put your HeyGen API key in the file used by the download step (fixed path in heygen/heygen-estudio-baixar.mjs; it only checks the status and downloads the video).

npx playwright open --user-data-dir ~/.cache/inemaccbot/perfil-heygen https://app.heygen.com   # login; or another dir in RTEX_HEYGEN_PERFIL
mkdir -p ~/projetos/openpcbotv2
echo "HEYGEN_API_KEY=sua-chave" >> ~/projetos/openpcbotv2/.env

Whisper (inemavox)

inematds/inemavox in ~/projetos/inemavox (or another path in CS_INEMAVOX). rtex tries the resident Whisper service on port 7871; if it isn’t running, it uses inemavox’s transcrever_v1.py with large-v3 (about 10 GB on an NVIDIA GPU). The resident Whisper’s Python interpreter comes from CS_WHISPER_PY; by default, it points to a chatterbox conda environment on Nei’s machine, so point it to yours.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/inemavox ~/projetos/inemavox
# dependencies: see the inemavox README
export CS_WHISPER_PY=/caminho/do/seu/python3

Node, ffmpeg, Python

Node 18+, ffmpeg with ffprobe, Python 3 with Pillow, numpy, and cairosvg, the DejaVu font (the fonts-dejavu-core package, used for the name label), and the logged-in claude CLI for the critic. Product logos (e.g., Claude’s logo in the sample script) come from an open package that ferramentas/logos.sh downloads once to ~/.cache/r7ex/lobe-icons; without it, the prints step stops.

git clone https://github.com/inematds/rtex
cd rtex
npm i
npx playwright install chromium
pip install --user pillow numpy cairosvg   # or inside a venv
bash ferramentas/logos.sh                  # logos -> ~/.cache/r7ex/lobe-icons
Xvfb :99 -screen 0 1920x1080x24 &   # only for the HeyGen studio
Shortest route, without the studio: if you already have a 16:9 (1920×1080) video of yourself or your avatar speaking the script, skip HeyGen entirely: python3 rtex.py fazer <pasta> --de seu-video.mp4. The video’s speech must match the script by at least 85% (the engine checks with Whisper). This route doesn’t require HeyGen, a key, or Xvfb. Whisper listens in Portuguese; for a reel in another language, use CS_LANG=en or CS_LANG=es.
Optional: enviar sends the video to Nei’s Telegram bot (bin/enviar_bot_v3.mjs), and fila adds the video to r7ex’s publishing queue (~/projetos/r7ex/fila/fila.py, or RTEX_FILA). On a machine other than Nei’s, stop at qa and publish however you prefer.
User guide · step by step

From topic to checked video

Each edit lives in edicoes/<data>-<slug>/. The fazer shortcut (step 9) runs checar, prints, avatar, cena, render, and qa all at once.

1

Create the edit you

Copy the sample script (one topic: “one agent and one folder per task”) into a new folder dated today. The sample’s sources were checked on 28/10/2026, and checar rejects sources more than 7 days old: replace them with your own in step 2.

python3 rtex.py novo minha-edicao   # -> edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao/roteiro.json
2

Write the script, with sources you

Check each number against its source on the day you write it down, and record it in fontes[] with valor and verificado_em. Then write the hook, the blocks (format in “The roteiro.json file”), the full spoken lines, and the hook question.

3

Validate and run the critic

checar applies fixed rules. critico asks a claude -p for a score through the subscription: it passes with no blocking issues, thesis (the hook) ≥ 8, average ≥ 7.5, and no block below 6. It saves critica.json.

python3 rtex.py checar  edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py critico edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
4

Approve and submit the avatar you

Submission only proceeds with written approval for this production in avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN (one avatar per reel). After submission, the spoken lines are frozen: text errors are corrected in the captions (the script’s trocas field), not with a second avatar.

mkdir -p edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao/avatar
echo "ok para este reel" > edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao/avatar/APROVADO_HEYGEN
python3 rtex.py avatar-enviar edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao   # writes avatar/video_id.txt
5

Capture page screenshots

Real screenshots of the cited GitHub pages, and the logos. Open and read the screenshots before continuing.

python3 rtex.py prints edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
6

Preview without the avatar optional

While the studio works, use a copy of the folder: synthetic timings, scene, and a few individual frames (in seconds). Find layout errors before the avatar arrives. The preview doesn’t call the studio or use another avatar.

rm -rf /tmp/previa && cp -r edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao /tmp/previa
python3 ferramentas/tempos_sinteticos.py /tmp/previa
python3 rtex.py cena /tmp/previa
node motor/render.mjs /tmp/previa --quadros 1,10,20   # -> /tmp/previa/q_1.jpg, q_10.jpg, q_20.jpg
7

Download the avatar and adjust the pacing

avatar-conferir checks once (ready or processing). avatar waits, downloads, timestamps the words with Whisper, stretches the speech to about 182 words/min (HeyGen speaks quickly), adds the silence before the maxim and the 9 s card.

python3 rtex.py avatar-conferir edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py avatar edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py avatar edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao --de avatar.mp4   # reuse an mp4
8

Scene, render, and QA

The stage at 30 fps, the avatar in the lower third, the music, and −14 LUFS in two passes; QA saves build/qa.json and the contact sheet build/folha.jpg with 16 frames.

python3 rtex.py cena   edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py render edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao   # -> video.mp4
python3 rtex.py qa     edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
9

Or do it all at once

checar → prints → avatar → cena → render → qa. It doesn’t run critico (step 3). Without --de and a ready avatar, it submits the video to the studio and waits up to about 290 min for HeyGen.

python3 rtex.py fazer edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py fazer edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao --de seu-video.mp4   # without the studio
10

Review, revise, and deliver you

Read the contact sheet and qa.json; ask someone who didn’t write the script to review it. The hook question makes no promises, so there’s nothing to prepare for people who comment. On Nei’s machine, enviar sends it to the bot and fila adds it to the channel queue (never direct upload).

python3 rtex.py enviar edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
python3 rtex.py fila   edicoes/AAAA-MM-DD-minha-edicao
The roteiro.json file

The script format, at a glance

Excerpt from the sample script, shortened (the // lines are not valid JSON; the complete file is in modelo/roteiro.json).

{
  "versao": "rtex/1", "tema": "Para de criar agente: um agente só e uma pasta por tarefa",
  "gancho": { "tipo": "contrario" },
  "isca": { "tipo": "opiniao", "pergunta": "Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa?",
            "opcoes": ["UM", "VÁRIOS"], "lado": "um agente só", "tensao": "agente" },
  "fontes": [ { "id": "skills", "url": "https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/agents-and-tools/agent-skills", "valor": "uma pasta por tarefa", "verificado_em": "2026-10-28" } ],
  "trocas": { "é cê cê": "ECC", "três peças": "3 peças" },
  "blocos": [
    { "tipo": "tese", "fala": "Se você cria um agente para cada tarefa, eu tenho uma má notícia: o fio que liga todos eles vai arrebentar.",
      "tela": { "linhas": ["PARA DE", "CRIAR AGENTE."], "destaque": "AGENTE" },
      "objeto": { "comp": "tese", "params": { "metafora": "fio", "nos": ["✉️ e-mail", "📷 Instagram", "📄 propostas"] } } },
    // ... authority (source: skills), problem ("not your fault"), turning point ...
    { "tipo": "metodo", "n": 1, "nome": "PROCESSO", "fonte": "skills",
      "fala": "Um: o processo. Peça para a IA te entrevistar, uma pergunta por vez, até ela fazer sem você.",
      "objeto": { "comp": "pasta", "params": { "titulo": "O processo" } } },
    // ... method 2–3, evidence, caveat, takeaway ...
    { "tipo": "cta", "fala": "Eu já escolhi o meu lado: um agente só. E você? Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa? Comenta um ou vários.",
      "objeto": { "comp": "isca", "params": { "em": "Comenta" } } }
  ],
  "publicar": { "descricao": "... Um agente só ou um para cada tarefa?" }
}

Main fields

tipo (the order of the nine blocks) · fala (written out in full, with no numerals or “!”) · tela (large text and highlight) · objeto.comp + objeto.params (what the stage draws) · n and nome in method items (matching the turn index) · fonte (an id in fontes). At the top level: trocas (how speech appears in captions), cores (colored words), ritmo, and publicar (title, description, queue channel). checar also requires: a valid gancho.tipo and the frame 0 headline with up to 6 uppercase words; objeto.params.metafora in the thesis; linha1 and linha2 in the maxim, each with up to 7 words; sources checked no more than 7 days ago; and in isca: a pergunta ending in “?” (up to 70 characters) and spoken in the CTA, 2 or 3 one-word uppercase opcoes, spoken in the CTA (for an open question: exemplo, the answer the hand types), lado, tensao (a word from the question that appears in frame 0 and returns at the end), and publicar.descricao ending with the question. The old fields isca.palavra and isca.entregavel cause an error.

Stage components (objeto.comp)

tese, contador, chat_riscado, indice (with progress pills), pasta, repo (real screenshot + stars), manchete, selo_prova, ressalva, maxima (typographic flash), and isca (the question, the yellow pill with options, and the comment box). The tese with metafora: "fio" draws the object that breaks in frame 0.
